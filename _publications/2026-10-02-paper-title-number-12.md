<h1 align="center">
  <a>Beyond Single-Model Segmentation: Consensus-Based Model Selection for Handling Manifestation Variability</a>
  <br/> 
</h1>

> This work investigates whether combining predictions from multiple specialized segmentation models improves robustness under such variability. We evaluate three architectures—U-Net, vanilla Vision Transformer (ViT), and Swin-Unet—and two aggregation strategies: prediction averaging and Simultaneous Truth and Performance Level Estimation (STAPLE). .
